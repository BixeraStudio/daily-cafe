A Love Letter to Food, Coffee & South Kolkata

The Story of The Daily

How Chef Urvika Kanoi created an intimate, sun-drenched European culinary escape in the quiet bylanes of Desapriya Park.

2019 Bringing Global Bistro Culture to South Kolkata
The Philosophy

Where Global Technique Meets Fresh Local Produce

Daily Café is located on Satyen Dutta Road, a small, historic lane near the arterial Rash Behari Avenue. Although it appears quite quaint and understated from the outside, the interior opens up into an airy, surprisingly spacious bistro bathed in natural sunlight.

Founded by Chef Urvika Kanoi, The Daily was born from a singular passion: creating a space where people can enjoy thoughtfully curated food made without shortcuts. We don't believe in industrial sauces or pre-packaged compromises. From our handmade tortellini and ravioli to slow-infused brown butter cappuccino foam, everything is prepared from scratch using the freshest regional produce.

In addition to our Kolkata sanctuary, our sister bistro in Mumbai — @cafeduco in Bandra — shares the same culinary ethos: playful, elevated comfort food, vibrant seasonal flavors, and boundless warmth.

What Drives Us

The Four Pillars of The Daily

The principles behind every plate, every cup, and every smile at 5 Satyen Dutta Road.

1. Fresh Local Produce

We source fresh vegetables, seafood, herbs, and dairy directly from regional farmers and artisanal purveyors, transforming raw local bounty into refined global dishes.

2. Curated Coffee Extraction

From our viral Brown Butter Cappuccino and slow-dripped Vietnamese iced brews to ceremonial grade Uji Mango Matcha, each cup is weighed, pulled, and textured with barista precision.

3. Handmade Pastas & Mains

Silky egg dough rolled by hand for forest mushroom tortellinis and ricotta ravioli, complemented by tenderloin steaks and fresh Kolkata Bhetki grilled in lemon herb butter.

4. Cozy Intimate Ambiance

Warm ambient lighting, curated vinyl and acoustic playlists, plush seating, and polite attentive service make every visit feel like a leisurely European vacation.

Cross-City Brotherhood

Our Bombay Amigo: Cafe Duco

Food connects cities. In Bandra, Mumbai, our partner bistro @cafeduco explores Latin American and European bistro dishes with equal vigor. This cross-pollination of recipes, culinary experiments, and seasonal specials keeps our menu constantly evolving and exciting.
