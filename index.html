Chef Urvika Kanoi's Vision

A Tucked-Away European Sanctuary in Desapriya Park

"Although it appears quaint from the outside, stepping into The Daily reveals a surprisingly spacious haven of natural light, Paris bistro chairs, and the intoxicating aroma of freshly ground espresso."

Founded by Chef Urvika Kanoi, The Daily serves global cuisine with a relentless devotion to fresh, sustainable local produce. Together with our Bombay sister bistro, @cafeduco in Bandra, we bring world-class European culinary technique and warm hospitality to South Kolkata.

Fresh Local Produce

Every pasta sauce, brioche bun, and compound butter is prepared fresh daily.

Single-Origin Coffee

Specialty arabica beans extracted with precision — from flat whites to brown butter brews.
