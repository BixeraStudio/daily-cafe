Landmark & Transit Guide

  • The Maharaja Tea Stall Landmark: Turn into the lane directly adjacent to the legendary Maharaja Tea Stall. The Daily is 50 meters down on your left.
  • By Kolkata Metro: Get down at Kalighat Metro Station (Gate 1 or 2). It's a quick 5-minute walk (450m) along Rash Behari Avenue toward Desapriya Park.
  • Parking: On-street parallel parking available on Satyen Dutta Road and adjoining Lake Market lanes.
